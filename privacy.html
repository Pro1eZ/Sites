Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

TrailRig is local-first: your data lives on your device, not our servers. Here's exactly what that means.

Overview

TrailRig is a local-first iOS application for bike component tracking and management. All data you enter — your profile, bikes, components, photos, service history, invoices, insurance records, and price watches — is stored on your device, and (if you enable iCloud Sync as a Pro user) in your own private iCloud account. We do not operate servers that store your personal data, do not create user accounts, and do not sell your data.

Data We Collect

We collect only the data you explicitly enter:

  • First name, last name, and nickname
  • Country, city, and optional street address (used only to estimate shipping costs in Search Parts)
  • Bike details, component specifications, and ride distances
  • Your rider weight, if you enter it for the Suspension Setup Assistant (used to look up recommended starting pressures)
  • Photos of your bikes and components (stored locally on your device)
  • Tire profiles per wheel (named tire sets with brand, size, casing, valve details, target pressure, weight, purchase info, and ridden distance)
  • Invoices and receipts you add per bike or component (attached PDFs or photos, vendor, date, and price)
  • Price Stalker watches (the product search terms and target prices you choose to monitor)
  • Insurance policy details per bike (insurer name, policy number, coverage amount, premium, renewal date) and any attached insurance documents (PDFs or photos)
  • Suspension damping profiles (named tunes with compression and rebound values) saved per fork or shock
  • All of the above syncs via iCloud when Pro iCloud Sync is enabled, stored in your personal iCloud account under Apple's iCloud Privacy Policy

Your GPS location is only accessed if you explicitly tap "Use My Location." Only the resolved city and country are saved — the raw coordinate is immediately discarded and never stored.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to display your Garage and component history, pre-fill search queries when you look for parts, and show accurate event times in your local timezone. No data is sent to us or any third party except what you explicitly initiate.

Notifications

Most TrailRig notifications are scheduled locally on your device — reminders for bike events, alerts when a component is due for service, and insurance renewal reminders (one week before a policy renewal date). These are scheduled entirely on-device and send no personal data to us or any third party.

Price-drop alerts (Price Stalker). Prices are checked on your device while you use the app. If you have parts on your watch list, TrailRig may also register those alerts for background delivery so you can be notified even when the app is closed. To do this, your device's push token and your watch list (the search terms and target prices you set — no other personal data) are stored on our server and re-checked periodically; when a price meets your target, a push is sent to your device via Apple's Push Notification service. You can stop this at any time by removing your watches or turning off notifications for TrailRig in iOS Settings.

Part Search & Affiliate Links

When you search for a part, your search terms (brand, model, spec) are sent to our search backend to retrieve results — no personal information is included. TrailRig may display affiliate links and earn a small commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you. Each shop's own privacy policy applies to purchases you make there.

Invoice Scanning & On-Device AI (Smart Scan)

When you scan an invoice or spec sheet to add parts, the image or PDF is read entirely on your device — the file itself is never uploaded, and it is discarded after parsing.

On an Apple-Intelligence-capable iPhone (iOS 26 or later), you can also use Smart Scan, which re-reads the invoice with Apple's on-device Foundation Models to catch parts the quick scan missed. This runs entirely on your iPhone — no text, image, or file is sent to our servers or to any third-party AI service, and nothing is stored off-device. Smart Scan is unavailable on devices that don't support Apple Intelligence; on those, the standard on-device reader is used.

Apple Health Integration

If you import ride distances from Apple Health, that data stays on your device and is never transmitted externally. TrailRig requests read-only access to cycling workout data only — it never writes to Apple Health, and Health data is never shared, sold, or used for advertising.

Feedback & Diagnostics

When you use Send Feedback, the email includes a short diagnostic log — recent app activity plus your bike/component names — to help us reproduce and fix issues. This log is generated on your device and is included only if you choose to send the email; nothing is uploaded automatically or in the background, and no diagnostics are collected on our servers. You can read or delete the message (and its attachment) before sending.

Data Storage & Security

Data is stored on-device using Apple's SwiftData framework with the iOS Data Protection API — encrypted when your device is locked. TrailRig Pro users may optionally enable iCloud Sync, which stores data in your personal iCloud account under Apple's iCloud Privacy Policy. We operate no external databases of your personal data.

Exported backups: when you export a backup via Settings → Backup & Restore, you choose its protection. Select “Encrypt with a password” and the file is encrypted (AES-GCM with a strong, password-derived key) — it can’t be read without your password, so it’s safe to store or share anywhere. If you choose “No password,” the file is unencrypted (though still protected on-device by iOS) — anyone with access to that plain file can read its contents, so keep it in a trusted location. Passwords aren’t recoverable, so don’t lose them.

In-App Purchases

TrailRig Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple, tied to your Apple ID, and shareable via Family Sharing. We never see or store payment information.

Tracking, Analytics & Advertising

This app does not track you across apps or websites, and contains no analytics SDKs, ad networks, or off-device crash reporting. No App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown because no cross-app tracking occurs.

Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You can access, correct, or delete all your data at any time via Settings → Delete My Account & Data. This permanently erases everything on your device and, if you use iCloud Sync, wipes the copy stored in your personal iCloud — so it is removed from all your devices. No request to us is required. Note that simply deleting the app does not remove data already synced to iCloud; use Delete My Account & Data first for a complete erase.

Children (COPPA)

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes & Contact

Updates to this policy are published here and included in the next app update. Questions? See our Contact page or email feedback@trailrig.app.
