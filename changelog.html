Version 2.25

2.25.59 (build 1258) New — October 2026

  • Insurance Dashboard — tap the shield in the Garage toolbar to see every bike's insurance status at a glance (insured / expiring soon / uninsured), each as a colour-coded pill with your chosen bike icon.
  • Personal Health & Accident Insurance — store your own health policy (insurer, policy number, coverage, premium, renewal date) on your profile, with renewal reminders a week before it expires.
  • Multiple documents per policy — attach several PDFs or photos to a bike's insurance or your health policy; tap any to view, swipe to remove. Each syncs and backs up individually.
  • Custom bike icons — pick an icon for each bike (🚵 🚴 🏍️ 🛵 🚲 ⛰️ and more) in Add/Edit Bike; it syncs across your devices.
  • Required field indicators — every form now shows a purple indicator on fields that must be filled before saving. Bikes, components, invoices, insurance — nothing silently saves half-empty.
  • Invoice validation — invoices now require a title, vendor, date, linked part, and at least one attached file before they save.
  • Smarter brand suggestions — every component form suggests the right brands for that part type. Product line names removed from suggestions.
  • Bike Birthday — set an "Owned since" date on any bike. On the anniversary each year, your garage card shows a birthday cake, the detail shows how long you've owned it, and you get a yearly reminder.
  • Keychain security — shared bike credentials are now stored in iCloud Keychain and no longer included in backup exports.
  • Service interval tracking — tap any service interval on a fork or shock to see km, hours, and time used since last service, colour-coded by how close to due.
  • Lake Placid 2026 — full 4-day schedule with official session times.
  • Events — event series now use their own names: Hardline, Rampage, Urban MTB, and MTB World Series.
  • Many sync, backup, and reliability fixes under the hood.

2.25.13 (build 1212) — October 2026

  • 🎂 Bike Birthday — set an "Owned since" date on any bike (tap Edit). On the anniversary each year, your garage card and bike detail show a birthday cake, the detail shows how long you've owned it ("Owned for 2 years, 3 months"), and you get a yearly reminder at 9am. Toggle in Settings → Notifications → Bike Birthdays.
  • Log Ride is now a full screen — opens as a navigation push instead of a sheet, keeping Notes and Elevation visible above the keyboard.
  • Manual Ride Elevation — manual rides now have Ascent and Descent fields (metres), synced via iCloud.
  • Bike cards refreshed — slightly shorter so more bikes fit on screen; text is whiter and more legible on both silhouette and cover-photo cards.
  • Brand autocomplete expanded — ~70 more brands added across all component forms and Add Bike.
  • Garage Toolbar preference — choose between a single menu or separate buttons for Swiss Knife, Cost Dashboard, and Weight Dashboard (Settings → Preferences → Units & Search).
  • Bug fixes & polish: TrailRig purple is now consistent everywhere; thin hairlines on scrollable pages are gone; "TrailRig Strava Club" link added in Settings → About.

Version 2.24

2.24.45 (build 1144)

  • Ride History elevation (ascent ↑ / descent ↓) now shows on its own line — long numbers like 12,450 m are never clipped.
  • Elevation now shows thousand separators (12,450 m instead of 12450m) in Ride History and on the bike card.
  • Ride History: long-press to enter multi-select mode, select individual rides or everything at once, then delete in one tap.
  • Older rides now automatically fill in heart rate, speed, elevation, and calories in the background — no need to open each ride.
  • Duplicate imported rides are cleaned up automatically; bike-card totals and service intervals now reflect the correct deduplicated distance.
  • Ride import button waits until heart rate data finishes loading before you can tap it, so HR is never missed on import.
  • Speed now shows both km/h and mph side by side.
  • Health lookback: choose 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, or a specific year when importing from Apple Health.
  • Service History timeline — tap ⋯ → "Service History" on any bike for a single chronological view of every service event across all components.

2.24.18 (build 1117)

  • iPad grid cards polished — outer corners now rounded with 16 pt padding so cards never touch the screen edges; text layout fixed so it stays correctly inset inside the card.
  • Swiss Knife now works as a standalone hub from the garage toolbar — open Torque Reference or the SAG Calculator without needing to open a specific bike first.
  • Shared specs sync between parts automatically — set the rear axle standard on your Frame and the rear Wheel fills in to match (and vice-versa); same for fork axle ↔ front wheel and BB standard ↔ bottom bracket.
  • Component forms now show persistent field labels ("Brand", "Model") so you always know which field you're editing.
  • App Clip: scan a published bike's QR code to instantly view its build sheet, km ridden, and last service dates — no install needed.
  • Share & Protect: new "Show service history & km" toggle so you control how much detail viewers see.
  • Cassette: smallest/largest cog steppers now auto-fill the Range field (e.g. "10-51t") — no more typing it separately. Road cassette ranges (11-28) are now in range.
  • Frame wheel size now covers the full range: 26", 650b, 24", 20", 16" added alongside 29", 27.5", 700c (shown as "700c (28")" for road/gravel bikes).
  • Brand autocomplete expanded: Superior, Decathlon, Rondo, Simplon, Veloflex, Classified, CeramicSpeed, and ~20 more across all categories.
  • Chain, cassette, and chainring service defaults are now discipline-aware — road and gravel get realistic km targets instead of flat MTB defaults.
  • Fix: Service History timeline crash when any entry had a non-zero km or hours value.
  • Fix: Tube puncture history now appears in the Service History timeline.

Version 2.20

2.20.88 (build 1057)

  • Data-loss fix: a bike created right after deleting all data could vanish on the next sync — now stays put.
  • Data-loss fix: pulling to refresh right after adding a bike could delete it before iCloud caught up — new bikes are protected until the upload lands.
  • Share TrailRig screen polished: cleaner Share link button, App Clip Code and QR code side by side.
  • Shared bike links now open directly in the app (trailrig.app/b/… deep links). Viewing a shared bike is free.
  • Scanning the promo App Clip Code when the full app is already installed now shows a welcome screen with "Open the app" and "Share TrailRig" buttons instead of doing nothing.
  • Profile sync: your name, nickname, location, and rider weight now sync correctly to a second device on first setup.
  • Settings navigation rewritten — Preferences, Notifications, Data & Backup, About & Privacy, and Pro all open reliably on iPhone and iPad.
  • Restore data-loss fix: restoring a backup can no longer be overwritten by a stale "account deleted" signal from another device.

2.20.44 (build 1013)

  • Invoice amounts are safe. Amounts typed with a comma (like "89,90") are kept exactly as you enter them — no more silent rounding or corruption.
  • Redeem Pro codes in-app. Tap Redeem Code on the Pro screen; Restore Purchases now reliably unlocks Pro after a promo code, with the "Welcome to Pro" celebration on first unlock.
  • Cleaner forms. Create Profile and Edit Profile now match — labeled fields, required-field markers, and safeguards so you can't accidentally blank out your details.
  • All-purple icons. Swiss Knife, Settings, and Preferences icons now match the app's purple theme throughout.
  • Better on iPad. Every screen — forms, bike detail, events, Swiss Knife, and more — now centers content in a comfortable reading column. Nothing stretches edge-to-edge.
  • Smoother onboarding. Welcome screen no longer flashes for returning users; you always land on your Garage after setup, even on a second device.
  • You can now set a personal photo as your bike's card background (Edit Bike → Garage Card).

2.20.25 (build 994)

  • Required fields now show a subtle purple accent bar when empty, so you can see at a glance what still needs filling in.
  • Brand validation: a gentle hint appears if a brand isn't in the known list — advisory only, never blocks saving.
  • Brand autocomplete updated with 100+ bike brands and 20–45 per component category — no more false warnings on real brands.
  • First-time launch shows a welcome screen summarising how TrailRig handles your data, with a one-time Terms and Privacy acceptance step.
  • App icon updated to the correct centered TrailRig logo.
  • Numeric fields (weight, pressure, invoice amount) now show a Done button on the keyboard.

Version 2.19

2.19.87 (build 956)

  • Apple Health is now opt-in with a clear "Connect to Apple Health" toggle in Ride Log (default off). Turn it on to import your cycling workouts — read-only; tap Disconnect any time.
  • Barcode/QR scanner improvements: reads labels right-side-up from your photo library, picks up Shimano box codes more reliably, and "Scan Again" now re-arms the camera instantly.
  • Events can now receive live schedule updates over the air — new schedules and time changes appear without waiting for an app update.
  • Tapping a notification bell in Events now enables notifications automatically once you grant permission.
  • PIN entry screen fits correctly in landscape on iPhone.
  • "Log Chain Replaced" now correctly resets the chain's distance to zero, matching how cassette and crankset already behave.
  • Part-distance tracking is now guarded by automated tests (~19 test cases) so it can't silently break in a future update.
  • Brand colours polished: TrailRig purple (#bf5af2) is now consistent across the tab bar, icons, and all UI controls.
