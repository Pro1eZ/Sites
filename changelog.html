Updated October 2026

What's new in TrailRig, newest first. Updated with each approved App Store release.

Version 2.25

2.25.13 (build 1212)

🎂 Bike Birthday

Set an "Owned since" date on any bike (tap Edit). On the anniversary each year, your garage card and bike detail show a birthday cake, the detail shows how long you've owned it ("Owned for 2 years, 3 months"), and you get a yearly reminder at 9am. Toggle the reminder off any time in Settings → Notifications → Bike Birthdays.

Log Ride is now a full screen

Log Ride opens as a navigation push instead of a sheet — the Notes and Elevation fields stay visible above the keyboard so you can actually see what you're typing.

Manual Ride Elevation

Manual rides now have Ascent and Descent fields (metres). Values sync to all your devices via iCloud.

Bike cards refreshed

Cards are slightly shorter so more bikes fit on screen. Text is whiter and more legible on both silhouette and cover-photo cards.

Brand autocomplete expanded

~70 more brands added across all component forms and Add Bike — boutique frames, gravity brands, droppers, tires, wheels, brakes, cockpit, and e-bike systems.

Garage Toolbar

A new preference (Settings → Preferences → Units & Search → Garage Toolbar) lets you choose between a single menu button or separate buttons for Swiss Knife, Cost Dashboard, and Weight Dashboard.

Bug fixes & polish

  • TrailRig purple is now consistent everywhere — tab bar icons, pickers, sliders, toggles, back buttons, component form icons, and segmented controls all use the same shade.
  • Thin hairlines above and below scrollable pages are gone (iOS 27 Liquid Glass scroll-edge effect, now hidden).
  • "TrailRig Strava Club" link added in Settings → About.
