🚀 Getting Started

What is TrailRig? 🚵

TrailRig is a free iOS app for mountain, road, and gravel riders who want to know their rig inside out — what's fitted, how worn it is, and what's due before the next ride. It has five tabs — Garage (track your bikes & components), Search Parts (find and compare spare parts across shops), Price Stalker (get pinged when a part drops to your price), Events (follow MTB race calendars — World Cup DH/XC, Crankworx, Hardline, Rampage and more), and Settings. Everything stays on your device by default — no account required. Pro users can turn on iCloud Sync to share their garage across their own devices. 🔒

Is TrailRig really free?

Yes — free to download and use, with no ads and no account. A few advanced features (like iCloud Sync and Price Stalker) are part of an optional one-time Pro purchase. No subscription, ever.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There’s no login, no sign-up, and no password to forget. Everything you enter lives on your device — your garage is yours alone. 🔒

Which devices does TrailRig run on?

iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.

🛠️ Maintenance & Wear

How does “km on this part” work now? (Automatic distance)

TrailRig now tracks how far you’ve ridden on each chain, tire, cassette, and chainring — automatically, from your ride logs. You no longer have to tap “Set install point” when you fit a new part.

  1. Fit a part (add a Chain, Tire, Cassette, or Crankset to a bike).
  2. Its distance counter starts from 0 km right away.
  3. Every ride you log on that bike adds its distance to the part.
  4. When you swap in a fresh part, open it and tap “Reset install point” so its counter starts over.
💡 The counter follows the bike’s total distance, so make sure your rides are logged (manually or via Apple Health) for the numbers to be accurate.
What does the cassette / chainring wear status mean?

Your cassette and chainring now show a wear status based on how far you’ve ridden them versus their typical lifespan (about 8,000 km for a cassette, 12,000 km for a chainring). It’s a guide, not a measurement — actual life depends on conditions, cleaning, and how worn your chain was.

  • 🟢 Good — plenty of life left.
  • 🟡 Monitor — past ~70%, keep an eye on it.
  • 🟠 Replace Soon — past ~90%, plan a replacement.
  • 🔴 Replace Now — at or beyond its expected life.

Want a different number? In the Wear & Distance section there’s an “Expected life” field — type your own km figure (based on your components and riding) and the status recalculates against it. Leave it blank to use the default.

To see it: open the Cassette or Crankset → the “Wear & Distance” section.
How do I record chain wear, and why does it warn me about my cassette?

A worn chain quietly grinds down your much more expensive cassette and chainring — so TrailRig warns you before that happens.

  1. Open your Chain → “Wear Status”.
  2. Tap “Log Wear Reading” and enter the figure from a chain-wear tool (e.g. 0.5%, 0.75%, 1.0%).
  3. The chain shows a colored status; at ~0.75% or higher, if your bike has a cassette or chainring fitted, a red warning appears telling you to replace the chain now to protect the drivetrain.
🔧 Rule of thumb: replace the chain around 0.5–0.75% wear and your cassette lasts far longer. Wait past 1% and you’ll likely need a new cassette too.
What is the health-check card at the top of my bike?

Every bike now opens with a quick health-check card so you can see at a glance whether it’s ready to ride. It shows:

  • An overall status — 🟢 Ride-ready, 🟠 Check soon, or 🔴 Needs attention.
  • Total distance, total ride time, and (if you’ve entered prices) the bike’s value.
  • Everything that’s due for service, plus your worst drivetrain-wear status — all in this one card.

The color reflects the most urgent thing — an overdue fork service or a worn-out chain will turn it red. Components that need attention (fork, shock, chain, cassette, chainring) also get a small red/orange dot next to their name in the list below. On Pro the card also lists any compatibility warnings (see below). In your garage list, each bike shows small drivetrain / suspension / tires dots so you can spot what needs attention without opening it — that part's free.

What are compatibility warnings? (Pro)

TrailRig cross-checks the specs you've entered on a bike and flags likely mismatches right in the health card. It currently checks:

  • Drivetrain speeds that don't match (e.g. an 11-speed shifter with a 12-speed cassette)
  • A cassette bigger than your rear derailleur can handle
  • Frame ↔ wheel size, and a mixed front/rear ("mullet") setup
  • Fork ↔ front-wheel axle, and frame ↔ rear-wheel axle
  • Freehub ↔ cassette, and rotor mount (6-bolt / Centerlock) ↔ hub
  • Bottom bracket ↔ frame shell, and a tire wider than the frame's clearance

Each warning includes a plain-language note on why it matters and how to fix it, and you can tap it to jump straight to the part. It only warns when both parts are filled in and clearly conflict — blank or unknown specs never trigger a false alarm, and equivalents like 700c = 29″ are treated as the same. A Pro feature.

How do service reminders work now that they run in the background?

With Pro and Service Reminders turned on, TrailRig checks your fork and rear shock service intervals once a day in the background — so you get reminded even if you haven’t opened that bike’s screen in a while. A service comes due on whichever happens first: ride-hours, distance, or time — so a bike you track by odometer still flags a suspension service (lower-leg / air-can around 500 km or 50 ride-hours; full service around 2,000 km or 200 ride-hours; or every 6/12 months). You can override any of these per part in the fork/shock Service Intervals.

  1. Get Pro.
  2. Go to Settings → Notifications and turn on Service Reminders — the app will ask permission to send notifications the first time (tap Allow).
  3. That’s it — when a fork or shock service comes due, you’ll get a “🔧 Service Due” notification.
Notifications start off so nothing bothers you until you opt in. Event Notifications and Service Reminders are separate switches — turn on whichever you want; whichever you enable first is the one that asks for permission. iOS decides exactly when background checks run (usually while charging on Wi-Fi), so timing can vary by a few hours — you won’t miss a due service.
Can I store my bike's insurance in the app? (Pro)

Yes — open a bike, tap the shield button, and record your policy (insurer, number, coverage, premium) plus attach the insurance document (PDF or photo). Set a renewal date and you'll get a reminder a week before it expires. It syncs across your devices. You can also export an Insurance Valuation PDF (from the Share menu) listing every component with its serial and value — ideal for a claim. Both are Pro features.

What's the QR code / theft protection? (Pro)
  1. Open a bike → Share menu → “Share & Protect (QR)”.
  2. Turn on Publish to create a private web page for the bike, and get a QR code to print or share.
  3. Choose what's public — just the basics, the full build, or add a contact/reward note (never your serial numbers or prices).
  4. If it's stolen, flip Report stolen and the page shows a clear STOLEN banner.
🏷️ Great for resale ("scan my build") or so a finder can identify and return your bike. A Pro feature.
What happens when I retire a worn part?

On a component (chain, tire, cassette, chainring, and more), tap “Retire this part” at the bottom of its form. It's archived to a Retired Parts section on the bike with its final distance, and removed from the active build (it goes to the Bin). Over time you build up lifetime stats — how many you've gone through, average km, and cost per 1,000 km. Retiring is free.

Can I add a whole bike from its invoice instead of typing everything? 🧾 (Pro)

Yes — Scan Invoice to Add Parts turns a bike invoice or spec sheet into a ready-to-add component checklist, read entirely on your device (nothing uploaded).

  1. Open a bike → Scan Invoice.
  2. Import a PDF (cleanest results), snap a photo, or pick up to 10 photos for a multi-page paper invoice — they're read together.
  3. Review the detected components (brand, model, specs), tick/untick what you want, and add them all in one go.

Smart Scan (on-device Apple Intelligence). On an Apple-Intelligence-capable iPhone (iPhone 15 Pro, any iPhone 16, or newer, on iOS 26), you'll also see a Smart Scan button that re-reads the invoice with Apple's on-device AI to catch parts the quick scan missed. It runs entirely on your iPhone — nothing is sent to our servers or any third-party AI. On devices that don't support Apple Intelligence, the standard on-device reader is used and Smart Scan simply doesn't appear.

💡 A clean PDF or spec sheet reads best — a straight-on, well-lit photo helps if you're using the camera. You always review before anything is saved. A Pro feature.

🔧 Garage & Components

How do I add my first bike?
  1. Open the Garage tab.
  2. Tap “+ Add Bike”.
  3. Give it a name (e.g. “Enduro” or the model), choose a type, and save.
  4. Open the bike to start adding components — Frame, Fork, Drivetrain, Brakes, and more.
💡 Add components as you go — even a rough setup is enough to start tracking service intervals.
What can I record for each component?

For every part you can log brand, model, install date, serial number, purchase price, and weight — plus a full service history so you always know what’s fitted and when it was last serviced. Multi-piece parts (like brakes or wheels) can even track each piece separately.

How do I scan a barcode or QR code?
  1. While adding or editing a component, tap “Scan Barcode / QR Code”.
  2. Point your camera at the code — or pick a photo from your library.
  3. TrailRig reads it and, for major brands (Fox, SRAM, Shimano, DT Swiss, Trek and more), auto-fills what it can.
  4. Review the details and tap Apply.
📷 No luck with a code? You can always type the details in by hand.
How do I add photos of my bike or parts? 📷

Open a bike or component, tap the photo gallery, and add pictures from your library or camera. They’re stored on your device and included in your backups. Handy for a before/after of a fresh build, or documenting a scratch before you sell.

Can I give each bike its own look in the garage? 🏔️

Yep. Open a bike → Edit → Garage Card and pick a silhouette backdrop for its card — snow-capped mountains, forest, rolling hills, a switchback trail, city skyline, and more. Leave it on Auto to match the bike type, or choose None for a clean card. Set the tint (purple or grey) in Settings → Preferences → Units & Search. Your choice syncs across devices and rides along in backups. Purely cosmetic — but your trail bike should look the part. 😎

Can I move a component to another bike? 🔄

Yes — swapping a wheelset or dropper between builds is one tap. Open the component, scroll to “Move to another bike,” pick the destination, and it moves over with its full history and photos intact (its ridden distance keeps counting on the new bike). If the destination slot is already filled, the old part is retired to the Bin so you don't end up with two forks on one bike.

What can I set for units and currency? 📏

In Settings → Preferences → Units & Search you can choose your distance unit (km / miles), component weight (grams / ounces), bike weight (kg / pounds), and your currency symbol for prices, garage value, and the Cost Dashboard. The currency setting changes the symbol only (no conversion), and is separate from Search Parts / Price Stalker, which use each shop's own currency.

Can I export or share my bike’s build?

Yes — open a bike and tap the Share button (top-right). Choose Build Sheet to export a clean PDF listing every component with brand, model, and serial number. You can share it with your mechanic, send it to a buyer, or keep it as a record. This is a free feature available to all users.

Pro users can also export an Insurance Valuation PDF (same Share menu) which adds purchase prices and a total value — ideal for an insurance claim.

How do I know which tools I need to service a part?

Open a component that needs servicing — a fork, rear shock, chain, cassette, crankset or brake — and scroll to “Tools for this job.” TrailRig lists the tools the job typically needs (for example a suspension pump and fork/shock tools for a fork; a chain tool, wear checker and degreaser for a drivetrain; a bleed kit for brakes). Tap any of them to jump straight into Search Parts and find it. It's a free feature for all users.

🔍 Search Parts

How do I search for a part?
  1. Open the Search Parts tab.
  2. Tap a category (e.g. Brakes → Disc Brakes) or type a term in the search bar.
  3. Tap any result to open it at the shop.
  4. Use Filters and Sort to narrow by brand, size, price, and more.
Why do the prices sometimes differ from the shop?

Prices shown are indicative — pulled from partner feeds and refreshed periodically. The amount you actually pay at checkout can differ because of shipping, promotions, or currency, so always confirm on the shop’s own page before buying.

Does TrailRig make money when I buy something?

TrailRig may earn a small affiliate commission if you buy through a link — at no extra cost to you. It’s how the app stays free and ad-free. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Why don’t I see results for every category?

Results come from partner shops for your Search Region (set in Settings). If a category is empty, that store may not stock it, or may not ship to your country — try a different term or adjust your region.

Search Parts is still growing: we're actively adding more partner shops and improving results, so coverage will keep expanding.

🔔 Price Stalker (Pro)

How do I start watching a part’s price?
  1. In Search Parts, find the part you want.
  2. Tap the purple “Stalk” button on that result.
  3. Set your target — a percentage off or an exact price — and save.
  4. It now appears in the Price Stalker tab, checked automatically. 🎯
💡 You can also open the Price Stalker tab and tap “Watch a Component” to add one manually. Up to 10 watches with Pro.
How often are prices checked, and how will I know?

Watched items are re-checked while you're using the app — when you open the Price Stalker tab, pull down to refresh, or when iOS runs a background refresh — and also periodically on our server so you can be alerted even when the app is fully closed. When one is at or below your target you get a notification on your device, and the item shows the cheapest in-stock price it found with a one-tap Buy link.

For closed-app alerts, your watch list (search terms + target prices) and your device's push token are stored on our server to run the checks — see the Privacy Policy. Turn it off any time by removing your watches or disabling notifications for TrailRig in iOS Settings.

Your watches sync across your devices. With iCloud Sync on, a watch you add on your iPhone shows up on your iPad (and vice versa), and deleting one removes it everywhere — all through your own private iCloud.

How do I pause or turn off an alert?

In the Price Stalker tab, tap the bell icon on a watched item to toggle its alerts on or off. To stop watching entirely, swipe the item to delete it.

⭐ TrailRig Pro

What do I get with Pro?

Pro unlocks Price Stalker, Deal Alerts (a push when a part drops past your discount threshold), Ride Log (import rides from Apple Health), Weight Tracking, Damping Profiles (save & switch fork/shock suspension tunes), Tire Profiles (save & switch tire sets — summer/winter — per wheel), Suspension Setup Assistant (weight-based starting pressure from your brand's tables + sag targets), Compatibility Check (flags likely part mismatches from your specs, with how-to-fix and tap-to-open), Service Reminders (get notified when your fork or shock needs service), Insurance Vault (store each bike's policy + document with a renewal reminder), Invoices & Receipts (keep receipts/invoices per bike and per part, with warranty reminders), Scan Invoice to Add Parts (photo/PDF of a bike invoice → a checklist of components to add, read on-device), Insurance Valuation PDF, QR Code & Theft Protection, Cost Dashboard (what you've spent per bike and across your garage), iCloud Sync, and Family Sharing 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 — plus every future Pro feature we add.

What does the Cost Dashboard show? (Pro)

It totals what you've spent — per bike and across your whole garage: total spend, spend by category, cost per km (for bikes with mileage logged), and your most expensive parts, including retired ones. It uses the purchase prices you enter on each component, shown in your chosen currency.

What is the Suspension Setup Assistant? (Pro)

On a fork or rear shock, tap Setup Assistant. Enter your rider weight (saved to your profile), pick your riding style (XC, Trail, Enduro, Downhill), and set your fork's stanchion size (32/34/36/38/40 mm) so the assistant picks the exact table for your chassis. It gives you a target sag % — converted to mm for your exact travel — and, for many forks, the official starting air pressure for your weight, shown in both psi and bar, straight from the manufacturer's own charts: Fox, Marzocchi, Öhlins, DVO, SR Suntour, X-Fusion and Formula (Marzocchi coil forks show the recommended spring; Cane Creek Helm uses its published formula), with a "never exceed" maximum-pressure note. Coil forks/shocks get a spring-rate calculator with a diagram of what to measure. You also get volume-spacer guidance and a direct link to your fork/shock brand's own official setup guide. It covers 11 brands: Fox, RockShox, Öhlins, Marzocchi, Cane Creek, DVO, Manitou, SR Suntour, X-Fusion, EXT and Formula. Sag is the real setup method — the manufacturer's own guide has the exact numbers for your model.

☁️ iCloud Sync & Backup

How does iCloud Sync work?

With Pro, your garage syncs automatically across your Apple devices through your own private iCloud account. Add a component on your iPhone and it appears on your iPad — no manual steps, no separate account.

I deleted a bike / emptied the Bin on one device — why is it still on my other device?

Deletions sync too, but they can take a little longer than edits. TrailRig cleans up deleted items and emptied-Bin items during a full refresh, which runs automatically about every 15 minutes while the app is open — so the other device catches up on its own within a few minutes. If you want it instant, pull down to refresh on the Garage (or Bin) screen on the device that's still showing the item; that forces a full refresh and clears it right away. (Both devices need iCloud Sync on and the same Apple ID.)

How do I move everything to a new phone?
  1. With Pro: turn on iCloud Sync and sign in with the same Apple ID on the new phone — your garage restores automatically.
  2. Without Pro: use a backup file (below) — export on the old phone, import on the new one.
How do I back up or restore my data? 💾
  1. Open Settings → Data & Backup.
  2. Tap Export Backup, then pick what to include — all bikes or just some, plus your profile, Price Stalker watches, and followed events (everything's ticked by default, so a full backup is one tap → Next).
  3. Choose password protection (encrypt it) or no password, and it saves as a single file to Files → On My iPhone → TrailRig, or share it to iCloud Drive / Dropbox / anywhere.
  4. To restore, tap Import Backup, choose your file, and pick exactly what to bring back — you can restore just one bike if you want.

A backup includes everything: bikes and their details, all components with specs and serials, service / pressure / chain-wear / battery history, damping + tire profiles, the retired-parts archive, insurance policies + documents, invoices & receipts, followed events, Price Stalker watches, your garage card designs, and every photo. 📦

💾 Your data lives on your device, so export a backup before switching phones or deleting the app — think of it as a spare tube: you only miss it when it's too late.
Are my backups encrypted? 🔐

Your choice, right when you export:

  • 🔒 Password-protected — the backup is encrypted (AES-GCM with a strong PBKDF2-derived key). Even if the file ends up on someone else's machine or a cloud drive, it's unreadable without your password. There's no recovery if you forget it, so store it safely.
  • 📄 No password — a plain file you can open and restore freely. It still gets iOS file-protection on your device, but once it leaves the app it's only as private as wherever you put it.

Old backups made before encryption existed still restore fine. Your on-device data and iCloud Sync are encrypted regardless (see "How secure is my data?" below).

🛡️ Privacy & Data

How secure is my data?

On your device: your data is protected by iOS Data Protection — it’s encrypted at rest, tied to your device passcode and Secure Enclave, so it’s locked whenever your phone is. 🔒
App Lock: if you set a PIN, only a secure hash of it is stored — in the device Keychain, never as plain text and never synced off your device. Face ID / Touch ID are handled entirely by iOS.
iCloud Sync (Pro): data syncs through your own private iCloud account, encrypted by Apple in transit and at rest.
Exported backups: you choose when you export — encrypt with a password (AES-GCM) for a file that's safe to store anywhere, or save it plain for easy access. See "Are my backups encrypted?" above.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. TrailRig has no servers holding your personal data and no account system. If you enable Pro’s iCloud Sync, your data lives in your own private iCloud — governed by Apple’s privacy policy, not ours.

Does TrailRig track me or show ads?

Neither. There’s no cross-app tracking, no analytics SDKs, and no ad networks. When you search for parts, only the search terms go to our backend to fetch results — with no personal information attached.

How do I delete all my data?

Go to Settings → Delete My Account & Data to permanently remove everything on your device. Because it’s all stored locally, deleting the app also erases your data. Full details in our Privacy Policy.
